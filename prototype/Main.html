Questions before you begin.

What do I need to get started?

Your PAN, a mobile number linked to your Aadhaar (we send an OTP to it) and a bank account in your name. Keep them nearby before you begin.

Why do you need my PAN and Aadhaar?

Every investor in India has to complete KYC, an identity check required by regulation. We use these details for that check.

The app is taking a while. Did it crash?

Probably not. Some checks take a few minutes. The app shows you where you are, you can close it, and we send a message when it is done.
