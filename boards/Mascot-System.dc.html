Usage rules

  • Ankur appears once per screen, at most 96px on onboarding forms.
  • Motion is a slow leaf sway only, and stops under reduced motion.
  • Every tap target is at least 44 by 44px, with 8px between targets.
  • The risk warning sits at the bottom of the first view on every money-adjacent screen.
  • Never: confetti, coins, countdowns, or Ankur beside a return figure.
  • Never promise an outcome or describe any product as free of risk, in any language. Compliance keeps the banned-word list.
